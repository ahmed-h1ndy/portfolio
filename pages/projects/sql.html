Customer Engagement & Behavior Analysis

How do students on an online learning platform engage with content, what do they buy, and where is growth hiding? I modeled the data in SQL and built a three-page Tableau dashboard to answer those questions for 35,000+ users.

  • SQL
  • Tableau
  • 35K+ users

Context

The dataset comes from an online data-science learning platform and covers student registrations, purchases, course ratings and daily watch time. The goal was to understand behavior across countries, measure learning engagement for free and paid users, compare course performance and find patterns in purchases and subscriptions.

My role

Solo project: data exploration, SQL modeling, dashboard design and the final recommendations.

Approach

  • Modular SQL with CTEs: a ratings CTE for rating counts and averages, and a minutes CTE for total and average watch time per course.
  • Reusable views: purchases_info calculates subscription start and end dates by plan (annual, quarterly, monthly); full_student_info joins student details, purchases and daily activity with onboarding and paid flags.
  • Analytical queries: top five countries by purchases and by watch time, and the strongest purchase months.
  • Tableau dashboard: three linked pages (Students Overview, Courses Overview, Top Courses & Onboarding %) with filters for registration month and user type.

Key insights

  • Purchases concentrate in English-speaking countries, which points to translation and localization as a growth lever.
  • India and Nigeria show high engagement but low conversion, a case for testing regional pricing.
  • Odd months outperform even months because of campaign timing. Marketing works, but revenue could be smoother.
  • Introductory courses dominate watch time, so structured learning paths could raise lifetime value.
