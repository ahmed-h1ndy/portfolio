E-Learning Onboarding Survey

Who signs up for a data-science learning platform, how did they find it, and what do they want to learn? A Tableau Public dashboard built on 937 onboarding survey responses from January to June 2023.

  • Tableau Public
  • 937 respondents

Context

New students on a data-science e-learning platform answer a short onboarding survey: where they live, how they heard about the platform, their career goal and what they want to learn. The aim was to turn those responses into a view the marketing and content teams could filter and act on.

My role

Solo project: survey data cleaning, dashboard design and publishing on Tableau Public.

Approach

  • Processed the survey responses to capture where learners come from, how they found the platform, their goals and topic interests.
  • Combined a total-students KPI, a world map of new users and a dual-axis chart of registrations against survey completion rate.
  • Ranked discovery channels, career goals and learning interests with sorted bar charts.
  • Added filters for country, onboarding status, paid or unpaid, subscription type and date range.

Key insights

  • The US and India are the largest sources of new learners.
  • YouTube is by far the top discovery channel, followed by Google.
  • "Become a data scientist" is the most common career goal, and data analysis and visualization is the most requested topic.
  • Survey completion ranged from about 67% to 83% by month, with May the lowest.
  • Recommendation: keep investing in YouTube and search content, and prioritize analytics learning paths.
