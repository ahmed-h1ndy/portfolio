YouTube's Top 1,000 Channels

What separates YouTube's biggest channels, and when did they break out? An interactive Power BI dashboard that traces the growth and reach of the top 1,000 channels from 2004 to 2021.

  • Power BI
  • DAX
  • 1,000 channels

Context

A public dataset of YouTube's top 1,000 channels, with launch year, category, subscribers, views and video counts. The question: what drove the growth of the platform's leading creators, and how does a channel's age relate to its reach today?

My role

Solo project: data preparation, measures, dashboard design and the written insights.

Approach

  • Built a single-page Power BI report, so the whole story fits on one screen.
  • Charted subscriber totals, total views and the number of channels by launch year to show how each generation of channels performs.
  • Added a category treemap to show where subscribers concentrate.
  • Headline KPI cards: most-subscribed channel (234M), average videos per channel (9.72K), average total views (10.15bn) and total subscribers across the top 1,000 (21bn).
  • Added a launch-year slider so viewers can focus on any period.

Key insights

  • 2014 was the breakout year: more of today's top channels launched then than in any other year, and they hold the largest subscriber total.
  • The 2012–2013 algorithm shift toward watch time lines up with the rise of long-form content.
  • Mobile access accelerated global growth, especially across India, Brazil and Europe.
  • Early adopters that evolved their formats kept their lead, while 2014 newcomers grew fastest.
  • Music and Entertainment hold the biggest share of subscribers.
